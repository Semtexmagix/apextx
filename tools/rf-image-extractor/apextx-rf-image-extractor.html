NOBLE NB4 / NB4+

RF Image Extractor

Unpack the RF module and receiver firmware from your own FlySky updater and copy it to the radio, ready for an in-radio update in ApexTX.

STEP 1

Your FlySky updater

Choose the FlySky Noble NB4/NB4+ updater you downloaded from FlySky. Nothing is uploaded; it is only unpacked on this PC.

STEP 2

Destination

Pick the radio's USB storage (connect it in ApexTX under System > USB), or a local folder.

    ApexTX RF Image Extractor v__VERSION__ — contains no FlySky files; you provide your own updater. The unpacked images are FlySky's and must not be redistributed.
